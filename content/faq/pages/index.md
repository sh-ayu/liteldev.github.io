## Where the answers come from

Most answers were collected from questions asked in the LeviMC community groups between 2023 and 2026, merged, and checked against each project's source code, documentation, and releases in November 2026. Any answer that could not be confirmed this way says so. Answers that no longer apply to the current versions were left out.

Found a mistake or a missing question? Every page has an **Edit this page** link, or you can ask in the [community](https://levimc.org/community/).
